Brand Guidelines — March 2026
01
Brand story
Benchlot is a marketplace and tool intelligence platform for premium used hand tools. "Kerf" is the cut a saw makes in wood. "Re" means again — a second cut, a second life for every tool.
What we are
A peer-to-peer marketplace where woodworkers buy, sell, and trade hand tools — planes, chisels, saws, mallets, measuring tools. Positioned as "Reverb for woodworkers."
ToolScan
AI-powered tool identification and valuation. Point your camera at any hand tool — we'll tell you what it is, what it's worth, and help you list it in seconds. Built for estate sellers and collectors alike.
Personality
Sturdy, confident, knowledgeable. Like a well-worn Carhartt jacket — not precious, not corporate, not trying too hard. Speaks like a peer to woodworkers and a patient guide to estate sellers.
Audience
Hand tool woodworkers, collectors, restorers (men 30-65 who follow Paul Sellers and Rob Cosman). Estate sellers inheriting tool collections. Newcomers looking for quality used tools at fair prices.

Usage guidelines
Do
Use Petrona 900 for the wordmark — always capital R, lowercase ekerf
Use Outfit for all body text, nav, buttons, and UI elements
Use Bone backgrounds — never pure white
Use Honey for CTAs with Dark Teal text
Use Spruce for secondary buttons with Bone text
Keep generous whitespace — let the tools breathe
Let product photos be the visual star — muted UI, vivid tools
Don't
Don't use pure white (#fff) or pure black (#000) backgrounds
Don't add an icon or symbol to the wordmark
Don't use Honey text on Honey backgrounds
Don't set the wordmark in anything other than Petrona 900
Don't use bright, saturated colors — keep everything muted
Don't use decorative or script fonts anywhere
Don't add drop shadows, gradients, or bevels to the wordmark
